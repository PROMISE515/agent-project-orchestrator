# Collaboratively solve a project problem

In Codex, invoke the Skill explicitly:

```text
Use $collaborative-problem-solve to work with me on this issue: checkout failures increased after a payment release. Do not make a final recommendation until you have separated facts from assumptions, offered reversible options, and shown me the smallest next test.
```

Sol owns the diagnostic process. It may call `luna_diagnostician` for narrowly scoped evidence, but the active project's Terra-Luna pod remains the primary project owner.
